YouTube → SkillSettings · v0.4

Turn videos into skills you keep.

Reads a YouTube transcript in your browser and pastes it into your own Claude or ChatGPT, ready to send. No server, no account, no API key.

How it works

  1. Hover over a thumbnail or the player and click the red ribbon. On a video page you can also press Alt+Shift+S or click the toolbar icon.
  2. Pick Claude or ChatGPT, and whether to create a new skill or update one you have.
  3. Press Open (or ⌘/Ctrl+Enter). Claude or ChatGPT opens in a new tab with the prompt pasted in; you review it and press Send.

Defaults

Opens the official website in a new tab and pastes the prompt into the message box. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

A language code such as en, tr or fr, for videos with several caption tracks.

Saved.

The skill

Results are more consistent, and prompts shorter, when your assistant has the youtube-to-skill skill installed.

  • Claude Code save as ~/.claude/skills/youtube-to-skill/SKILL.md
  • Claude app put SKILL.md in a folder named youtube-to-skill, zip it, upload it where your account manages skills
  • Others copy the folder into the tool’s skills directory (Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI…)

Privacy

On YouTube the extension reads the transcript from your browser. On claude.ai and chatgpt.com it acts only in a tab it opened itself: it pastes the prompt once and reads nothing else. Settings are stored in Chrome sync storage. No analytics, no server, nothing sent anywhere until you press Send.
